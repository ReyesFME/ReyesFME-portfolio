Synopsis
        An online persona, Cecil, was once a successful vlogger / influencer that eventually disappeared from the spotlight. This once hobby turned into an obsession, a problem, something that even his friends noticed and got concerned about, Cecil then instead asks them for advice on how to turn his life around, he was tipped about an app called “faME”, a streaming platform that focuses on live gifts and interaction with the content creator and viewers directly. He then almost immediately jumped into the app’s algorithm and found a new ‘trend’ where he’ll ask the viewers to ‘dare’ him and he’ll do it live, adhering to what the viewers specifically ask for. At first, the dares are normal, often harmless, and victimless. But these types of dares did little to none to improve his status. He even got negative reviews, plunging him into depression. With his popularity’s steady decline, it further led to fewer viewership and less sponsorship, affecting him financially. When he was on the brink of losing all the assets he founded, he grew desperate, he needed to make a comeback in his socials to boost his status and be launched back into fame. He thought of something, an absolute rule that the viewers’ requests are to be fulfilled because he made up a ‘bidding’ system where viewers could pay and bid for specific tasks they want him to do, the more dangerous, the higher the bid should be. Every time he tries a trend, his personality and dignity are slipping away, adapting to the wants of the viewers, completely disregarding his own. This constant exposure to the digital world made him a multifaceted person, from which he made a ‘face’ for each trend he hopped on to, after all, he needs to be someone the viewers want to be otherwise they wouldn’t support or watch him at all. Eventually, these ‘faces’ rumbled and got his head distorted, and he found himself lost in the trend he’s currently doing, like a sleepwalker being awakened mid-dreaming. He got knocked back to reality and found himself in a chaotic environment where he doesn’t even know himself anymore. He tries to remember what he’s doing such dares. Was it “for fame, for money, or for fun?” That's the ultimate answer Cecil was trying to answer as he's dissociating back into reality. As he was in the deepest point of his life yet, the friends he abandoned did not abandon him. They instead found where Cecil is shooting his dangerous stunt and decided to help him turn his life around.
