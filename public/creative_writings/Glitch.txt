When the two worlds collided


There had been two virtual worlds, one set in a regular world and the other set in a post-apocalyptic one. All these are implemented in the minds of the two parts of the group of people. However, two curious minds managed to transcend the fiber of reality in which they are fakingly living.


They become friends because when observed, they're similar in a way that made them excluded from the rest. And since they're from two different worlds, they see each other as some kind of static or projection but as their minds rip through the fibers, they both become too real. When the one operating this bizarre overlapping world, they're beyond angry at this unexpected development, they wanted to run things the way they want, but their persistence demolished that. To lessen further damage, they wanted to get rid of these two. But before they could get their hands on them, the two killed each other first.
