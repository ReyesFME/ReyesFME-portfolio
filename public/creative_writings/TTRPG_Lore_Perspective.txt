The birth of the twin divinities and the archipelago of Maca
The archipelago was a plain land before the birth of the twin divine rulers, Bathala and Bathrawarka. Their great powers are rooted from the pure energy of the archipelago, molded by the creation of complex life and desire-to live. These brothers were conceived by the magic and complexity of nature, allowing them to exist in the form of benevolence. These twins then created its first inhabitants, creating complex lifeforms similar to theirs and then they created more to make a paradise. Together, they created the
archipelago that they first called as 'Maca', meaning heaven but was also later dubbed as "Talang nag sibol sa Mapiang Banwadagat" and it was a magical archipelago where both mortals and divinities lived together for eras on end.



Despite being twins, their birth created a very different surge of divination, Bathala encompasses warmth-offering kindness and divine protection to everyone and everything. But his twin, Bathrawarka was born with wickedness and cunningness, encompassing the dichotomy of Bathala-darkness. Despite differing ideologies, they both wanted to be a benevolent ruler of the archipelago.



One day, Bathrawarka announced that he wanted to rule alone, and to keep his brother from 'pampering' their subjects. Offended, Bathala accepted his challenge. They decided to battle it out for full control-to which Bathala won. Seeing his brother bitter and heartbroken, he decided to compromise by dividing the rule between them. The light and the dark thus creating the cycle of day and night. However, Bathrawarka decided he wanted full control, without being the shadow of his brother. He devised a plan to engulf the world in eternal darkness, drawing powers from the world beyond this one, the Kasanaan or the
equivalent of hell. His meddling with the universe's dark strongholds enraged Bathala and he threatened him to be imprisoned if he didn't stop. Bathala called upon the powers of the entire pantheon under his rule to definitely seal his own brother.



Behind the scenes, Bathrawarka had established his own main pantheon, the 7 moons, though he kept their existence hidden. He made them do his bidding sneakily, without alerting Bathala or any of his pantheons.



Bathrawarka however, instead of mobilising his pantheon, he relied on blind obedience of mortals and divinities, doing his biddings for him-these subjects were the ones who felt the unfairness of the whole world, they agreed to the belief that "magical and mortals shouldn't live together because it establishes the unfair nature of being normal and powerful", ultimately, it became his greatest weapon because
Bathala refused to harm his subjects, even if that means they didn't follow him anymore. Bathrawarka had been ordering his pantheon to gather the necessary ritual items and sacrificial necessities for his dark plan.



However, the rest of Bathala's pantheon urged him to do action, otherwise the archipelago would be thrown into chaos. His compassionate nature was exploited by his brother, the gradual fall of Bathala's rule cracked and shook the entire archipelago, this moment of weakness was seized by Bathrawarka and he managed to perform his ritual, ultimately separating the dimension of mortality and divinity. But

Bathala's last act was to stop this separation, sacrificing himself to secure and prevent anymore, but the large scope and great damage this ritual had done, Bathala had a hard time managing it so he asked for help with his pantheon. Together, they created the 3 major protectors hailing from Selurong, Mairete, and Ophir; these mighty protectors along with Bathala, managed to hold the world for centuries to come. This managed to balance out their divine prowess. Batahala gave up his powers to hold the world together while his twin was seeped out of his prowess from that large scale ritual. In a few more millennia, they were stagnant and unable to use their prowess.




The widescale ritual broke the archipelago of Maca, making some islands sink and more particularly, the region of Ophir was turned into a desert due to it being the heart of the dark ritual-the light and water was seeped out, leaving the region dry. Cracks and fissures riddled the archipelago throughout its entirety, reminding the people of divine wrath. To this day, these cracks became a hard reminder of such immense power from the forgotten gods.


Their inactivity made their subjects forget their existence, and eventually, the concept of deities slowly
disappear...but was not entirely gone.




Character Lore

Kael Sadiri - Gahumagia "from the word kahel and sadiri an ilocano word for intelligent"
His family's name was Sadiri, his name Kael came from the day he was born-a bright,
warm orange sky greets the day as his cries first echoed as he was cradled in his mother's arms. However, this premonition of a remarkable child being born suddenly became a curse because his mother knew-that her child is a Gahumagia. His strong Kalag is warm, warmer than the sun itself. The array of orange skies confirms her suspicions, the gods
gave another gahumagia...In his early years, Kael showed no signs of being a Gahumagia, it seems that his powers were kept locked as intended but by the time he reached 12, his powers slightly shows and it gives fear in the heart of his mother, Dalisay. In their hometown in Wararka, gahumagias are typically trained as warriors, serving for the monarchy until they die. Rumors have it that once a gahumagias swore an oath to the king, they'd be used and disposed of like rusted weapons. She can't let that happen. Dalisay
packed minimal, like she's just going for a short trip to avoid suspicions. She urged Kael to pack too and said that they're just going out for a trip. The trip itself is treacherous, her frail body can be felt like it's giving up yet she can't quit, she can't handle another loss of a loved one and not be able to do something about it. Her husband, Kalis, was also a gahumagia but upon exhausting his power, he was 'killed in action' as per the report of the king's guards. She travelled all the way to Lunob Awan, seeking sanctuary. When they reached the sanctuary, Kael was filled with questions as to why, upon being safe, Dalisay told him why. Kael was furious and wanted to really know what happened to his father but his mother was hesitant, she said that Kael's power is still too weak to even try and combat the Wararka royal family.


As Kael grew up, he knew he had powers but even he himself can't control and unlock its
true potential. His mother then suggested for him to pursue his curiosity and unlock his

capabilities, there is a region who teaches gahumagias like him-a school in Hikmarum, the magic capital of the north archipelago.



Haliya Sugbu - Animato "masked moon goddess" in Visayan mythology and sugbu "to wade into the water" or "to dive into water," stemming from a Proto-Philippine word (sug(e)bu), or it can refer to "scorched earth" or "great fire". (girl)


Haliya was born in Shivi-al. From birth, she was one with the warriors. For a while, her
strength seems normal for a typical Shiv, but when she started 'commanding' the seas and taming the flames, the entire settlement of the Shiv questioned her 'natural strength'. Their elder instructed her to go on a perilous journey north, just beyond the thunderous clouds of Taika Oblak. The elder was strict with the timeline; she had to arrive within a week, precisely, otherwise her journey would be useless. In reality, the elder was trying to make her go to the Didicas islands while it was still visible; if she missed her deadline, the Didicas islands would soon submerge and would not show themselves for an indefinite amount of time. On her journey to the far north, she was accompanied by anitos, helping or playing tricks around her. Despite the various nuisances, she managed to reach the far north and was instructed to manifest and control the waters as she sailed. To which she barely managed to tame. She arrived shortly at Didicas islands by the sixth day. The anitos
revealed that she was an 'animato' and the final test by the council of the Dalaketnon is to be held shortly. After completing the trial, she went home proudly, waiting to announce her 'passed' status and her heritage-she was ready to share the tale, but what greeted her was the ravaged village of her settlement. The surrounding area was literally scorched earth, and there were traces of the ritual abomination of monsters.



Sulà - Babaylan (guy)
He was the babaylan of the Batuk-an Patik, his years of service honed his healing and divination magic skills to their utmost precision and mastery. However, there have been several environmental problems recently: earthquakes, storm surges, and the slow and
gradual decay of crops from their neighboring forests. Being their tribe's babaylan, he was asked to do some kind of clairvoyant magic in hopes of finding answers to why they are experiencing these disasters. During his ritual, he used his divination magic, searching for answers from anitos and nature alike. What he saw was so devastating that he couldn't even say it to the tribe. He just said
"We...I have to do something..."


A few days later, he prepared many protective spells and blessed many relics and talismans to keep his tribe safe while he prepared for a journey. His goal was to reach Kayo Punlaan or the "Sacred Tree" in hopes to find more and deeper insight from it. But his suspicions
were just confirmed by the sacred tree...The end is beginning, a certain group of people are
making the gears of the nefarious plan to turn. He has to do something about it.

Salaknib - Katalonan (salaknib is an Ilocano word for "shield") (guy)
The impact of the dark plan was felt even by the Katalonans in Hanunoo. Salaknib was one of the high priests and the tremors of the quakes, the imbalance between the climates and decay of nature is becoming more and more apparent. He suspects that the followers of Bathrawarka are behind it so he decided to experiment. He compared the length of day and night. If the world is balanced, there should be an equal amount of it because that's how Bathala left the world-in proper balance. When he conducted it, the night was longer by an hour, it confirmed his suspicions and fear. He mobilized his faction, to sleuth and be more alert. He knew something was wrong, but thinking where to actually start looking for answers is a problem.



Talao - Pajareador (guy)
A hunter in Sanghaya forest. During his recent hunts, he noticed that some plants are in the state of decay or near decay. He knew that something was wrong but he couldn't pinpoint it. Upon returning from one of his hunts, he reported his findings to their settlement leader. The elders were shaken from this news, they feared that the 'balance' was being tainted.
They instructed Talao to keep a look out for suspicious people wandering in their lands as well as those sleuthing around Sanghaya and Mt. Pulag.



Writing and Spoken Language in this TTRPG
For this TTRPG, this is how the writing script works:
The baybayin and Hamunoo scripts act like common writing scripts, widescale use and everyone can read and write it. Despite the varying language and dialects across the archipelago, most people can translate it to baybayin/Hamunoo-based scripts-a shared written system that transcends dialectal differences.


Baybayin: mainly used by coastal, and sea-faring people. Inspired by how the waves work.
Syllable-based, written from left to right horizontally, mimicking the literal sea horizon. Baybayin is the de facto script, however, there are regional influences and creation of another script.

Hamunoo: mainly used by mainland/in-landers. Main inspiration is baybayin but also created specific characters for this variation.
• written left-to-right but with a vertical orientation, mimicking the orientation of the trees.
